## The affine baseline — finally quantified

MTP.md:180 called the VQ prefill tax "unquantified" since it was written.

    397B-2.2 vs spicyneuron affine 2.6bit, 2-node, 9k prefill
    before today   338 tok/s   82% of affine (411)
    after today    356 tok/s   87% of affine

NOT bit-matched (VQ 112 GB vs affine 121 GB) and the affine run had MTP off
while VQ had it on — so the PREFILL ratio is solid, the decode ratio is soft.

**Why exceeding affine is physically available:** VQ moves ~7-8% FEWER bytes.
If prefill were purely bandwidth-bound VQ would be ~8% FASTER; it is 13%
slower, so the gap is decode work not hidden behind memory traffic. Not a
fundamental disadvantage — an unhidden cost.

## Facts that should shape the next attempt

- The fused kernel is NOT faster than legacy at small NSUB / large OUT
  (in=640/out=2560, NSUB=80): 28.4% of Flash-2.1's decode work moved onto it
  and changed nothing. At NSUB=320 it is ~1.3x. MECHANISM UNMEASURED.
- RTILE is TOPOLOGY-dependent, and topology must be stated on every kernel
  result from now on. Two of four measurements were nearly reported wrong.
- Ring concurrency is NOT interconnect-limited: 6 concurrent = 4.47x over
  40 Gb/s RDMA vs 4.72x over TCP. A 0.4% difference. RDMA changed nothing.
- 397B on M4-solo batches BETTER (2.11x vs 4.47x at 6 concurrent) but its
  base request is 3.6x slower (112 GB model on a 128 GB box, 17% free), so
  the ring still wins on absolute wall clock: 62.7s vs 107.5s for 6.
